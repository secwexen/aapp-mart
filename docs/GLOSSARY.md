# AAPP-MART Glossary

This glossary defines common cybersecurity and AAPP-MART terminology used throughout the repository.

## AI-Powered Attack Path Prediction (AAPP)

The process of analyzing security and environment data to predict plausible paths an adversary could follow to reach a target or achieve an objective.

## Multi-Agent Red Team Simulation (MART)

A simulation approach in which multiple autonomous agents model adversarial behaviors and coordinate within configured scenarios to evaluate security posture.

## Adversary Emulation

A cybersecurity assessment approach that models adversary tactics, techniques, and behaviors to evaluate security controls within an authorized environment.

## Attack Graph Analysis

The analysis of graph-based relationships between assets, vulnerabilities, and potential attack transitions to identify and assess plausible attack paths.

## MITRE ATT&CK Alignment

The mapping of modeled adversarial behaviors and attack paths to relevant tactics and techniques in the MITRE ATT&CK knowledge base to provide standardized security context.

## Threat Model

A structured representation of assets, adversaries, trust boundaries, and attack surfaces used to reason about security risks and defensive priorities.

## Reconnaissance

The information-gathering phase in which adversaries collect details about targets, systems, users, and services to identify opportunities for exploitation.

## Attack Path

A sequence of steps or transitions an attacker may take across systems, identities, or vulnerabilities to reach a target objective.

## Privilege Escalation

The process by which an attacker acquires higher-level permissions or access rights than originally assigned, enabling broader control or lateral movement.

## Lateral Movement

The technique of moving between systems or environments after initial access to expand influence, reach sensitive assets, or maintain persistence.
